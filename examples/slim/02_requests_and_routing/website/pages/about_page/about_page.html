About

This page is routed by hand, in example_site/urls.py:

path('about/', AboutPage.as_view(), name='about')

Every other page declares its own route with @register_url and is included with one line, path('', PageRoutes.urls()). The two mix freely: a hand-routed page can live anywhere, and reverses by the name urls.py gives it, here reverse('about'), while decorated pages reverse as reverse('website:<name>').
